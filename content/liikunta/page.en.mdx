### TKO-äly's regular sessions

TKO-äly organises a regular session at Kumpula Unisport (Block A) every Wednesday at 18.00. You can vote for the sport in the poll on the [Telegram channel](https://t.me/tkt_liikunta) until the previous Tuesday evening.

Bring water, indoor sports shoes and a lock for the locker door. You can rent indoor shoes and a racket at Unisport if needed, and rent or buy a lock.

After the session you can go to the sauna at Unisport.

### Sports in university leagues

TKO-äly has taken part in university leagues in, for example, football, futsal, floorball and sähly when there has been interest and a team has been put together. If a league interests you, contact TKO-äly's sports officer or the board. Putting a team together on your own initiative is also welcome.

In the summers TKO-äly has taken part in the football tournament between
organisations arranged by Matlu, with varying success. In the winters kyykkä has
been played and the [Academic kyykkä World Championships](http://www.kyykka.fi)
in Tampere have been attended.

We also occasionally organise excursions to get to know different sports. Traditionally TKO-äly has, among other things, pulled rope, played kyykkä and visited Megazone, but fencing and archery have also been tried. Once again, if a sport interests you, contact the sports officer or the board.

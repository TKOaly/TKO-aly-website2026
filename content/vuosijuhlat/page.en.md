## Tips for the annual celebration

The annual celebration (vuosijuhlat) is the most elegant celebration of an academic organisation, and taking part requires a little preparation in advance, although in the case of our own association the celebrations have generally been on the more relaxed side. Of all the association's events it is perhaps the most festive and special, which often shows in the event's higher than average price. The celebration starts with a cocktail reception where the participants can present their greetings to the organisation celebrating its anniversary. Representatives of other organisations and partners attending the celebration may also remember the organisation with a gift along with their greetings.

The actual celebration begins after the cocktail reception, during which a three-course dinner is eaten and songs are sung. After the dinner the celebration culminates in academic dances. The organising team usually also arranges a dance practice for those interested in academic dances. There is usually also some kind of afterparty. On the day after the celebration a so-called sillis (herring brunch) is held, an informal event where you can swap your formal wear for everyday clothes or even overalls.

All members and friends of TKO-äly are invited to the annual celebration. The participants include students from freshmen to later-year students, alumni, department staff, representatives of other organisations and partners, etc.

## Dress code

For the annual celebration you dress more formally than for sitsit. Possible outfits include a tailcoat, a dark suit or a long evening gown, and academic decorations. Instructions on wearing academic decorations can be found on [TKO-äly's own pages](/yhdistys/tunnukset). TKO-äly does not set norms for dress etiquette regarding gender or other personal experience.

## Behaviour

Arrive on time, and if you are celebrating with an avec, it is customary to arrive at the venue together. At the celebration the seats are named, and the seating order can be found on the seating map at the venue. You stand by your seat until the whole party is present, then sit down and the celebration can begin. It is polite to introduce yourself to table companions you do not know. Eating is usually not started until the whole table has received their meal. During speeches or songs you do not clink cutlery, leave the table or talk unless this is absolutely necessary. At TKO-äly's celebrations the ban on leaving is not followed quite strictly, but it is still worth making sure you do not disturb the other guests by leaving during songs or speeches or by making a commotion as you leave.

The annual celebration always has a song leader, who starts the songs and goes through the practices of the celebration at the beginning. Usually you can suggest songs by telling your wishes to the song leader. At a suitable moment the song leader gives you permission to present your request or start the song if you do not want to do so yourself. In that case it is also polite to introduce yourself to the guests. Singing skill or musical talent does not matter at all. Songbooks with the words are handed out at the seats; the tune is usually familiar or easy to learn if you listen for a while and then join in.

In general, a good tip is to watch what others do if you feel unsure.

## A few keywords:

- _avec_ the person you sit next to at the celebration.
- _plaseeraus / table order_ the planned seating order of the celebration. Usually announced on a seating map attached to the wall of the venue or displayed in some other way.
- _sillis_ an informal event held on the day after the annual celebration, more relaxed than the actual celebration, for the guests and for others. Despite its name, sillis serves more than just herring.
- _skoolaus_ raising drinking cups with the table company.

## Telegram

The association's [general discussion rules](https://tko-aly.fi/keskusteluohjesaanto) must be followed on all of TKO-äly's discussion channels.

### tkt-announcement

The best way to keep up with TKO-äly's activities is to follow the Telegram [tkt-announcement channel](https://t.me/tktannouncement).

### TKO-äly2025

TKO-äly's official discussion channel [TKO-äly2025](https://t.me/Tekis2025) contains discussion about everything under the sun.

### Other Telegram channels

TKO-äly has many official and unofficial Telegram channels. They are listed [here](https://fuksiwiki.tko-aly.fi/Keskustelukanavat).

## Mailing lists

You join a list by sending a message to <code><list name>-join at tko-aly.fi</code>, for example <code>jasenet-join at tko-aly.fi</code>. The subject and content of the message do not matter.

You leave a list in the same way by sending a message to <code><list name>-leave at tko-aly.fi</code>.

### Member list

TKO-äly has an official mailing list for its members: jasenet at tko-aly.fi. The list is used to announce TKO-äly's activities, the association's meetings and occasionally other matters concerning computer science students.

The name of the list is <code>jasenet</code>.

### Recruitment list

TKO-äly's recruitment list is used to pass on information about open jobs and announcements of thesis and internship positions.

Messages to be sent to the list should be sent to yritysvastaava at tko-aly.fi.

The name of the list is <code>rekry</code>.

### Free-time list

At vapaa-aika at tko-aly.fi there is a more casual announcement list. Emails are sent there at a lower threshold than to the member list.

The name of the list is <code>vapaa-aika</code>.

The purpose of Ruokavälitys (RV) is to offer small snacks and drinks to poor souls exhausted by studying late in the evening, as well as other nice things to nibble on throughout the day.

Ruokavälitys is a volunteer-run, non-profit coffee fund that operates on a nest egg once placed there by TKO-äly. Cleverly cheating or abusing the "system" therefore harms only yourself and the other snackers.

Ruokavälitys is available to all **TKO-äly members**.

### What RV contains

The products of Ruokavälitys are located in Gurula. Candy and other dry goods are on the shelf, frozen goods, not surprisingly, in the freezer, and soft drinks in the soda cabinet with a window. Delicious Gurula coffee, tea and cocoa are also part of the RV selection. You can prepare hot drinks yourself by boldly using Gurula's coffee maker and kettle.

**Help is needed!** If something essential is missing from Gurula, such as coffee, filter bags, oat milk or dish soap, go and buy the supplies from your favourite shop and make a [kulari](https://tko-aly.fi/kulari) (expense claim). You are a good person and you get the money in your bank account after a couple of weeks.

### Paying

Paying for the products is done independently with the virtual till. The virtual till runs on the computer in the corner of the desk. An account is created in the virtual till and money is loaded to it either with a cash deposit or a bank transfer. The account is created by typing your own freely chosen username at the login prompt and then answering the questions about a password and your university ID.

Once you have created your account in the virtual till, buying products with the till's barcode reader is simple and fun: log in, read the barcode, press enter or read the 'LOGOUT' barcode. There are separate barcodes for bottle returns, coffee and other products without barcodes on the wall and taped to the desk.

#### Cash

You can load cash to your virtual till account by slipping your money into the RV till through the slot on top of the Grey Cabinet (the black metal cabinet) and entering the command 'd <amount>' (e.g. 'd 15,00') at the virtual till prompt. There is no change. You can ask someone in Gurula to change money for you, but you should not count on that.

#### Bank transfer

An active snacker should load a larger sum by bank transfer in one go. Deposit a suitable amount to the **Ruokavälitys account FI05 7997 7991 9503 25**. A reference number is not needed, but please include your RV username in the message. You **still have to record the amount** to your virtual account yourself using the command 'd', exactly as when paying with cash.

**Remember to keep your account balance positive!** New food cannot be bought before the old has been paid for. The RV trolls may from time to time tape a wall of shame of the worst freeloaders to the wall.

You can also check your account balance and purchase history [online](http://rv.tko-aly.fi).

### RV officers & wishes

Ruokavälitys is run by RV officers, whom you can reach by email at rv miukumauku tko-aly.fi.

Wishes for RV's content can be put on the RV wiki, that is, an A4 sheet taped to the door of the overall cabinet in Gurula. Wishes are granted if the wholesaler's selection and the brokers' busyness allow it.

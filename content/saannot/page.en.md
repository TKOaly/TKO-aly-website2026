**These rules were approved by the Finnish Register of Associations on 22 October 2025.**

_This is an unofficial English translation. In case of any discrepancy, the Finnish version of the rules prevails._

### § 1 Name and domicile

The name of the association is TKO-äly ry and its domicile is Helsinki.

### § 2 Purpose

The purpose of the association is to act as an advocacy organisation for computer science and data science students at the University of Helsinki in educational and social policy matters. In addition, the association seeks to promote students' team spirit and leisure activities.

### § 3 Realisation of the purpose

The association takes care of developing studies and improving the conditions for studying, in cooperation with the parties responsible for computer science and data science teaching at the University of Helsinki, through initiatives, statements and work in various working groups. The association organises parties, evening events and excursions, as well as cultural, sports and recreational activities.

To support its activities the association may engage in publishing, sell advertising space, make sponsorship agreements, sell products, organise lotteries after obtaining the relevant permit, and, when necessary, undertake other measures that promote the purpose of the association. The association may own real and movable property and accept donations and bequests.

### § 4 Members

The members of the association are ordinary, external, supporting and honorary members. A degree or postgraduate student at the University of Helsinki who studies or has studied computer science or data science may be accepted as an ordinary member of the association.

Any degree or postgraduate student or staff member of the University of Helsinki may be accepted as an external member. A private person or a legal entity may be accepted as a supporting member.

Ordinary, external and supporting members are accepted by the board of the association on application. The association may invite as an honorary member a person who has served the association's aims with merit, if at least 3/4 of the voting members of the association present at an association meeting support the board's proposal for an honorary member. Membership rights already acquired are retained.

### § 5 Membership fees

A membership fee is collected from the members of the association, excluding honorary members. The membership fees of different member groups may differ in amount. The membership fee period begins on 1 August and ends on 31 July.

The membership fee may also be paid at once for several membership fee periods, if the spring meeting of the association so decides. Paying for several periods at once may cost a proportionally different amount per year than paying for one period.

### § 6 Rights of members

All ordinary members have the right to vote at the association's meetings. Other member groups do not have the right to vote. All member groups have the right to speak at meetings. A member has the right to complain about the association's activities to the board of the association. A person dissatisfied with a decision of the board has the right to appeal the matter at an association meeting.

### § 7 Resignation and expulsion

A member may resign from the association by submitting a written notice of resignation to the board or its chair, or by announcing it at an association meeting. A member is considered to have resigned if they have not paid their membership fee for the current period by 1 December. The board of the association may expel a member who has not paid their membership fee for the current period by 1 December.

An association meeting may, by a 3/4 majority of the votes, expel for a fixed period or permanently a member who by their conduct has significantly hindered the association's activities. The person expelled is informed of the decision personally.

### § 8 Board

The board consists of a chair, a treasurer and a secretary, and in addition at most 11 other board members. The board is elected at the autumn meeting and its term is the calendar year. An association meeting may supplement the board during the term, until the end of the current term, in the manner provided in § 11, paragraph 5.

All board members must be ordinary members of the association. At its first meeting the board elects from among its members a vice chair, who acts in place of the chair when the chair is prevented from performing their duties.

A board meeting has a quorum if at least half of the number of board members are present, including the chair or the vice chair.

The board may, for a justified reason, restrict a member's right to participate in events or other activities organised by the association if the member has significantly hindered the association's activities or has repeatedly or seriously behaved disruptively towards others in the association's activities. The restriction cannot concern the member's rights mentioned in § 6, and its duration is at most 6 months at a time. The board has the right, when necessary, to later revoke the restriction or change the decision on its duration, but even then the continuous length of the restriction cannot exceed six months.

### § 9 Duties of the board

The duties of the board are to

- take care of the association's appropriate activities and handle current matters,
- maintain the membership register,
- be responsible for the management of the association's funds and other property,
- draw up the budget and the action plan,
- take care of the official representation of the association,
- convene the association's meetings,
- organise the association's meetings and implement the decisions made in them,
- draw up the financial statements and the annual report
- and supervise compliance with the rules.

### § 10 Operating and financial year

The association's operating and financial year is the calendar year.

### § 11 Association meeting

An association meeting has a quorum if it has been convened in accordance with the rules. The invitations and the agenda for association meetings must be delivered at least seven days before the meeting to the email list directed at the association's members. The board may decide that a condition for participating in an association meeting is registration for the meeting by a certain date at the latest, which may be two weeks before the meeting at the earliest. In that case the invitations and the agenda must be delivered at least seven days before the last registration date.

It is also possible to participate in an association meeting by post or by means of a telecommunication connection or other technical aid during or before the meeting, if the board or the association meeting so decides.

By decision of the board, the right to vote at an association meeting may be exercised through a proxy. The same proxy may represent, in addition to themself, at most one member.

The association meeting is organised by electing a chair, a secretary, vote counters and minutes inspectors for itself.

An association meeting may elect new members to the board if the board has fewer members than the autumn meeting has decided. At least 10 ordinary members of the association must then be present at the meeting. The election of board members must be mentioned in the meeting invitation.

### § 12 Spring meeting

The following matters are handled at the spring meeting, held between 1 February and 30 April:

- the financial statements and annual report of the previous term and the statement of the operations auditors are presented,
- a decision is made on adopting the financial statements and on granting discharge from liability to those accountable,
- the permitted membership fee periods payable at once are decided,
- the amounts of the membership fees are decided,
- other matters that arise are handled.

The board must deliver the financial statements to the operations auditors at least three weeks before the spring meeting.

### § 13 Autumn meeting

The following matters are handled at the autumn meeting, held between 1 October and 31 December:

- the chair of the board is elected
- the treasurer of the board is elected
- the secretary of the board is elected
- the number of other board members is decided within the limits of § 8
- it is decided whether other officials of the association, in addition to the chair, treasurer and secretary of the board, automatically belong to the board
- the officials of the association are elected
- the other members of the board are elected
- two operations auditors and two deputy operations auditors are elected
- the budget for the following year is decided
- the action plan for the following year is decided
- the remunerations of the auditors and of the board members are decided
- other matters that arise are handled

### § 14 Extraordinary meetings

An extraordinary association meeting must be convened if at least one tenth (1/10) or 20 members entitled to vote request it in writing from the board for a matter they have stated, or if the board or an association meeting considers it necessary.

### § 15 Handling of matters at an association meeting

All members of the association have the right to speak at association meetings. Only the ordinary members of the association have the right to vote. A member of the association may ask the board to put a matter on the agenda of an association meeting by submitting the request in writing to the board in time for the matter to be included in the meeting invitation. A matter outside the agenda may be taken up for handling if at least half of the voting members present at the meeting support the proposal.

Voting at the meeting is held openly, unless a member entitled to vote demands a closed ballot. Elections and votes on the expulsion of a member are always held by closed ballot. In elections the single transferable vote method described in the association's voting and election rules is used. In matters other than elections, the matter is decided by a simple majority of the votes. In the event of a tie the chair's vote decides, but in elections the decision is made by drawing lots.

A person dissatisfied with the decisions of the meeting may have their dissenting opinion recorded in the minutes.

### § 16 Signing for the association

The name of the association is signed by the chair or the treasurer alone, or by the vice chair and the secretary together. The board may authorise an ordinary member of the association to sign the name of the association alone.

### § 17 Amending the rules

A matter concerning amendment of the rules must be approved at two consecutive association meetings held at least two weeks apart. A proposal for amendment approved by at least a 2/3 majority at the first meeting must be approved unchanged by at least a 2/3 majority at the latter meeting. At least 10 ordinary members of the association must be present at both meetings. The amendment of the rules must be mentioned in the meeting invitations.

### § 18 Dissolution of the association

The association may be dissolved if, at two meetings held at least a month apart, at least 3/4 of the votes cast support the proposal for dissolution. At least 20 ordinary members of the association must be present at both meetings. The latter dissolving meeting of the association decides on the use of the remaining funds of the association to promote the purpose mentioned in § 2 of these rules. The dissolution must be mentioned in the meeting invitation.

### § 19 The TKO-äly foundation

The association may collect money for the establishment of a foundation and make donations to a foundation. Money may be collected and donated only for a foundation that significantly promotes the interests of the association's members. A matter concerning fundraising for a foundation and the possible establishment of a foundation must be approved at two consecutive association meetings held at least two weeks apart. A proposal approved by at least a 2/3 majority at the first meeting must be approved unchanged by at least a 2/3 majority at the latter meeting. At least 10 ordinary members of the association must be present at both meetings. Matters concerning the foundation must be mentioned in the meeting invitation.

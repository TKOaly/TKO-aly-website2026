TKO-äly ry is the subject association of computer science and data science students at the University of Helsinki. The association was founded in 1988 and currently has about 900 members. TKO-äly actively promotes the interests of computer science students and at the same time offers services for both leisure and studies.

For anything related to cooperation, feel free to contact the "corporate relations officers":#yhteystiedot at yritysvastaava at tko-aly.fi, or by phone using the numbers at the bottom of the page.

## Forms of cooperation

### Partnership

A partnership is the most versatile and significant way to be visible and to support TKO-äly's activities. Partnership agreements are always made individually and can include, for example, company visits, visibility on the website, visibility in TKO-äly's magazine Readme and theme evenings. A partner is seen and heard by computer science students all year round!

### Annual celebration sponsorship

TKO-äly's annual celebration is a yearly academic table party whose participants range from freshmen who have just started their studies to graduates and even professors. In addition to other visibility, the main sponsor of the annual celebration gets a speech at the main celebration. The celebration has about 100 participants, and more at the larger celebrations held every fifth year. The main celebration is held in a Helsinki restaurant, and the afterparty is often in the Alina hall of the Uusi ylioppilastalo. The annual celebration is an excellent way to be visible especially among slightly older computer science students.

### Overall advertisements

Overall advertisements are an easy and good way to be visible in student circles. Advertising on TKO-äly's overalls guarantees visibility among students for several years to come. We get new overalls every year.

### Company visits

We also organise traditional company visits, or "excus", for our partner companies. An excu usually includes a presentation of the company's activities, former computer science students telling what it is like to work at the company, a task that requires some brain activity, and of course food, drinks and sauna.

### Other forms of cooperation?

Our forms of cooperation are not limited to the above. Other potential forms include theme evenings on the latest technologies, supporting membership and sponsoring other kinds of events. We are also happy to hear your ideas!

## Recruitment list

Job advertisements from TKO-äly's partner companies are sent to the recruitment list. The recipients of the list are computer science students and graduates interested in jobs. The list is an excellent way to reach computer scientists interested in job opportunities! We also send announcements of thesis and internship positions to the list.

Send your recruitment advertisement to **yritysvastaava at tko-aly.fi**, and we will find out how your company can best benefit from our recruitment list.

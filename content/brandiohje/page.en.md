## Name

The name is always written with a lowercase ä: "TKO-äly" or "TKO-äly ry". There is no full stop after ry. In Finnish, inflected forms are written with a colon after ry ("TKO-äly ry:n, TKO-äly ry:llä") and directly to the proper name without ry ("TKO-älyn, TKO-älyllä"). In more informal contexts the nickname "Tekis, tekis" can also be used.

## Logo

<img src="https://www.tko-aly.fi/attachments/files/405/Logo-yellow-on-black.png?1723129014" width="200px" />

The TKO-äly logo is a black circle with a yellow border. Inside the border, the text "Studentes datalogiae universitatis Helsingiensis" ("Students of computer science at the University of Helsinki") runs around the circle in large letters. Inside the text there is a second yellow circle, in which the name of the organisation is stylised on two lines, "TKO" "äly".

The colours of the logo are black #000000 and yellow #fff500.

The logo has different versions for different backgrounds. The basic logo (black background, yellow text) is used in most contexts, and it stands out well as the TKO-äly brand.

The second most common is the black version, where the background is either white or transparent and the text and borders are white or black. In this form the logo is particularly well suited, for example, to official attachments or documents printed in black and white. This form is also often used in secondary Telegram groups, for example in the officials' group.

The white colour is slightly impure #f7f7f7.

You can find the logo files on the [admin wiki](https://wiki.tko-aly.fi/TKO-%C3%A4ly-logo) (sign in with TKO-äly credentials) or by asking the TKO-äly secretary. The page also has the logo in an unofficial 3D form as a GLTF file.

### The logo's safe area and its parts

The TKO-äly logo has no precisely defined safe area, but for visual balance it should not touch any other element directly at its edge. The TKO-äly logo should never be forced into a square shape, and the stylised "TKO äly" text part should not be used on its own. The TKO-äly logo always includes all the elements: two circular borders, the Latin text with the star element, and the stylised "TKO äly" text part.

When printing, take the printer's resolution into account and make sure the Latin text is legible.

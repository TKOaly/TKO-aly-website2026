README is the small but mighty magazine of our subject association. README deals with topics on a wide scale, for example related to studying, computer science and free time. We publish articles about the latest news of the association and the department, as well as lighter content. All issues, old and new, can be read as PDFs at the bottom of this page (the issues themselves are in Finnish). More texts can be found on [our blog](https://blog.tko-aly.fi).

The magazine is constantly in need of new ideas and writers - everyone is welcome! It is amazingly easy to join the editorial team, and you can contact the editor-in-chief by sending an email to paatoimittaja at tko-aly.fi.

## Vallilan Virus (2002–2005)

Vallilan Virus was our student magazine from 2002 to 2005. The name was changed to README in 2006 when the campus moved from Vallila to Kumpula. Vallilan Virus covered the same topics as README, but was a bit lighter and more humorous.
